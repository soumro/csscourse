📖 Understanding Flex‑wrap

flex-wrap controls whether flex items are forced onto a single line or can wrap onto multiple lines. When combined with flex-direction, it defines the flex flow (shorthand flex-flow: direction wrap).

  • nowrap – all items stay on one line, even if they overflow or shrink.
  • wrap – items wrap onto new lines in the cross‑axis direction (top to bottom for row, left to right for column).
  • wrap-reverse – same as wrap but lines are placed in reverse order (bottom to top for row, right to left for column).
🎯 When wrapping is enabled, align-content controls how the multiple lines are spaced. Use it to distribute lines evenly or align them as a group.
