4. Bonus: timing-function & delay

Hover → bounce effect

transition-timing-function controls the acceleration curve. Common values: ease, linear, ease-in, ease-out, ease-in-out, or custom cubic-bezier().

transition-delay adds a wait before the transition starts. Example: transition: all 0.3s 0.5s; (wait 0.5s then animate).

✅ Best Practices

  • 🔹 Use specific properties instead of all to avoid unintended animations and improve performance.
  • 🔹 Keep durations short – most UI transitions feel best between 0.2s and 0.4s.
  • 🔹 Combine with transform and opacity for hardware‑accelerated, smooth animations.
  • 🔹 Test on hover, focus, and active states to ensure accessibility.
  • 🔹 Use will-change sparingly to hint which properties will animate (can improve performance but may increase memory usage).
  • 🔹 Always provide a fallback for older browsers if needed, though transitions are widely supported.
