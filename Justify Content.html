🎯 CSS `justify-content`

Align items along the main axis – in Flexbox and Grid.

1. Flexbox: justify-content

Item 1
Item 2
Item 3
justify-content: flex-start;
💡 In Flexbox, justify-content aligns items along the main axis (horizontal when flex-direction is row, vertical when column).

Flexbox column

justify-content aligns the entire grid horizontally inside the container.

🧠 In Grid, justify-content only works when the total grid width is less than the container width. For aligning individual items inside a grid cell, use justify-self or justify-items.
