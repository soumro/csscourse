5. Best Practices & Performance

  • 🔹 Use shadows sparingly – too many or large blur can reduce readability and increase render cost.
  • 🔹 For large text, subtle shadows improve contrast and depth without distraction.
  • 🔹 Multiple shadows can be combined, but too many layers may impact performance, especially on animations.
  • 🔹 Animating text-shadow is possible but can be heavy; consider using transition sparingly.
  • 🔹 Always ensure sufficient contrast between text and its shadow (and background) for accessibility.
