5. Best Practices & Performance

  • 🔹 Use shadows sparingly – too many can overwhelm design and hurt performance.
  • 🔹 For animations, prefer box-shadow transitions; but be aware that changing shadows can be expensive. Consider using will-change: box-shadow; for smoothness.
  • 🔹 Combine with transition to create interactive hover effects.
  • 🔹 Use inset for inner shadows to create pressed or inset effects.
  • 🔹 Multiple shadows can be used to mimic complex effects like neon glow, borders, or 3D depth.
  • 🔹 When using large blur or spread, the shadow may extend far; ensure it doesn't overlap important UI in undesirable ways.
