📖 Deep Dive

🎯 animation-direction

  • normal – plays from 0% to 100%.
  • reverse – plays from 100% to 0%.
  • alternate – forward then reverse each cycle.
  • alternate-reverse – reverse then forward.

📌 animation-fill-mode

  • none – no styles applied outside animation.
  • forwards – retains last keyframe values.
  • backwards – applies first keyframe before delay.
  • both – both forwards and backwards.

⚡ animation-timing-function

  • ease, linear, ease-in, ease-out, ease-in-out
  • steps(n) – discrete steps (stop‑motion).
  • cubic-bezier() – custom curves.

⏯️ animation-play-state

  • running – plays normally.
  • paused – freezes the animation.
💡 Shorthand order: animation: name duration timing-function delay iteration-count direction fill-mode play-state;
Example: animation: slideMove 1.2s ease-in-out 0s infinite alternate forwards running;

🖱️ Interactive: Play-State on Hover

Hover to pause

Move your mouse over the box – animation pauses (animation-play-state: paused). Leave and it resumes.

.box:hover { animation-play-state: paused; }
