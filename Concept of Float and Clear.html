📋 Quick Reference

PropertyValuesEffect
floatleft | right | noneElement moves to side, inline content wraps around.
clearleft | right | both | nonePrevents element from being placed next to floats.
Clearfix::after { content: ""; display: table; clear: both; }Forces container to contain floated children.
